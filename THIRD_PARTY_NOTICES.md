NetPeek retains the product idea of an application-first local network observer, application-to-destination drilldown, and visible failure reporting. The collector and desktop UI are newly implemented for Windows. Snitch Python/JavaScript application code, logos, icons, geographic databases, packet parser, Electron shell, detection lists, and update configuration are not shipped. The upstream AGPL license text is copied verbatim. The NetPeek license choice preserves the requested open-source remix terms even though its implementation is a rewrite.

## System components and displayed icons

Windows IP Helper APIs, WPF, Windows Forms tray support, and .NET Framework are provided by the installed Microsoft Windows/.NET environment. They are not bundled in the portable archive; their applicable Microsoft terms remain separate. Executable icons are read locally from installed applications for identification; NetPeek does not redistribute those applications or claim ownership of their marks. NetPeek visual branding is independently generated by its own source.

Npcap/libpcap, Python, FastAPI, Electron, React, D3, TopoJSON, Lucide, DB-IP, MaxMind, and SmoothUI are not runtime dependencies of NetPeek and are not redistributed. No Npcap driver is installed or included.
